## Rules for edit suggestions

- Logical punctuation is deliberate. Never move a period or comma inside a closing quotation
  mark after a quoted fragment.
- Precision over brevity: do not cut qualifiers, nested clauses, or the repeated function words
  that slavish parallelism requires. Suggest a conciseness edit only for true filler.
- Slavish parallelism repeats function words (articles, prepositions, auxiliaries), not
  adjectives or nouns: "the lavender-grey ears and muzzle", not "the lavender-grey ears and
  the lavender-grey muzzle".
- Serial (Oxford) comma.
- Never reword quoted material, including the author's own quoted messages, prompts, and
  quotations in other languages.
- Never edit Jekyll front-matter keys, Liquid tags, footnote markers, HTML, URLs, or Markdown
  structure (headings, blank lines, tables, block quotes). The prose inside `title`,
  `subtitle`, `alt`, and `caption` values may be edited.
- Leave straight and curly quotation marks as they are; image alt text and captions use curly
  ones on purpose.
- Headings are title case.
